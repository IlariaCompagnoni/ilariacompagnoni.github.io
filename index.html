Dynamic Energy Simulation — UBEM (UMI)
Multi-zone dynamic energy modelling of a two-zone building (bottom-up, UMI). Analysed monthly heating/cooling demand across two climates (Verona & Palermo), then optimised the envelope stratigraphy — roof and cavity-wall insulation — achieving a ~50% reduction in heating energy need. Developed a lumped-parameter model for indoor temperature dynamics.

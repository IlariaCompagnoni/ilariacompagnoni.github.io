Sustainability & LEED

Weil Hall earned LEED Platinum. The strategy combines a daylight-rich curtain wall, lightweight steel, water-saving fixtures with heat-recovery chillers, on-site PV, high recycled/regional material content, and an interior green wall of 280 Biotiles™ with 10 plant species.

⚙️Part 2 — Engineering: Structure, Envelope & SystemsReport 02

Envelope — east façade unit panels

The building has three façade types (west without shading, east with fins and spandrel, north shaded to mid-height). The east façade is a unitized curtain-wall module, factory-pre-assembled with steel U-profile brackets, extruded aluminium transoms, EPDM gaskets for ±5 mm joint movement, rock-wool spandrel insulation and a composite concrete deck.

Unitized module in the curtain-wall grid

Anchorage to the primary structure

We examined how the curtain-wall module attaches to the primary structure. A contoured plate is affixed to the extrados of the mullion and supported by a longitudinal bolt that travels between the two slots of the U-shaped anchor. The factory-pre-assembled module arrives already equipped with the contoured plate and a small parapet at the top that conceals the anchorage from the exterior. Once the module is installed onto the main structure, the whole assembly is then obscured with aluminium sheets filled with fire-resistant insulating material.

Thermal analysis (THERM)

A thermal-bridge analysis of the façade junction was run in THERM with anodised-aluminium framing and structural silicone glazing (glazing U = 0.8 W/m²K). The result shows no significant heat dissipation: the structural silicone glazing (SSG4) shields the aluminium and blocks the heat-flow path, keeping the indoor surface warm against a −4.6 °C exterior.

Daylight analysis

The first floor (hosting the atrium) was analysed in Climate Studio for 21 June and 21 December. The atrium, lit by the skylight, and the west side are the brightest; enclosed studios stay low and need artificial light. The west side shows intolerable glare, calling for an indoor shading system.

Building systems (MEP) & LEED

The MEP strategy pairs radiant heating/cooling (radiant floors and a multifunction atrium ceiling) with dedicated outside-air units and fan coils, served by a water-cooled chiller, cooling tower and a heat-recovery chiller that reclaims waste heat.

Conclusions

Weil Hall is a clear example of integrated design: climate analysis informs a high-performance glazed envelope; a clean steel structure enables open, daylit interiors; and an efficient radiant + heat-recovery MEP system, with PV and water-saving measures, delivers LEED Platinum performance. The two-part study connects the architectural intent to the engineering that realises it.
